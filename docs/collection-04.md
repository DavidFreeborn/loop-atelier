# Collection 04 and the quiet studio

## Scope and plan

The studio should show the artwork, the controls needed to change it, and its mathematics. Remove promotional prose, repeated names, collection divisions, captions, ornamental labels and routine visible status messages. Retain the dark ground. Put all studies in one alphabetical selector. Replace the field guide with a short Sources tab containing an explanation and an actual bibliography.

Preserve playback, phase scrubbing, seed, deformation, exposure, duration, palette, applicable particle count, recipe import/export, PNG settings, fullscreen, reduced motion and accessible error reporting.

1. Simplify the source interface and its responsive layout. Verify existing studies and controls before adding more.
2. Build six independent mathematical studies: hyperbolic tessellation, quasiperiodic phason motion, phase singularities, topology-changing level sets, weighted cellular geometry and Milnor fibres. Keep exact mathematics separate from finite numerical approximations and artistic lighting.
3. Review multiple phases, variation settings and seeds. Inspect complete motion. Reject illegible, redundant or poorly framed candidates and improve them before publication rendering.
4. Integrate the new modules through the existing shader contract. Update source packaging, the flat inline selector, the native-video gallery, recipes and metadata.
5. Check actual shader evaluation: finite nonnegative radiance, unwrapped time periodicity, determinism, seed/variation response, and representative sampling. Independently verify the mathematical claims used in the explanations.
6. Render final 2,400-square PNGs and 1,440-square, 30 fps movies. Decode every final asset and inspect encoded motion and colour. Rebuild the standalone studio and archive with source, documentation, manifests and checksums.

Acceptance requires both mathematical validity and an effective moving image. A complicated equation alone is insufficient. Existing artwork and saved recipe formats remain supported.

## New study documentation

The final definitions and their numerical limits are documented in [geometry-04.md](geometry-04.md) and [mathematics-04.md](mathematics-04.md). The interface contains only the concise construction note and formula for the selected work; the detailed production record belongs in the toolkit.
